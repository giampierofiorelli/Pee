# Mission (owner, 2026-10-21)

The original Italian text is authoritative. The English translation below is provided for convenience.

## Original text (Italian, verbatim)

Progetta da zero un universo semantico scientifico generale, capace di rappresentare, acquisire, comprendere, collegare, verificare, aggiornare e riutilizzare conoscenza scientifica.

Il sistema dovrà successivamente costituire il fondamento di un consulente scientifico indipendente, capace di comprendere obiettivi umani, investigare fenomeni e soluzioni, interpretare evidenze e valutare prodotti o interventi.

Deve poter apprendere concetti e domini completamente nuovi, riconoscere conoscenze già acquisite e trasferire principi scientifici quando le condizioni lo consentono.

Il primo obiettivo obbligatorio è progettare l’universo semantico stesso.

Studia, senza assumere un’architettura predefinita:

* che cosa costituisce un significato e come viene identificato;
* come vengono rappresentati concetti, entità, processi, fenomeni, eventi, proprietà e relazioni;
* come funziona la composizione dei significati;
* come vengono rappresentati contesto, tempo, cambiamento, condizioni, quantità, negazione, possibilità e incertezza;
* come si distinguono conoscenza, osservazione, ipotesi, spiegazione, causalità, evidenza e inferenza;
* come un universo può acquisire nuovi concetti, rivedere conoscenze precedenti e svilupparsi senza perdere coerenza;
* come rappresentare anche la conoscenza metodologica, cioè la conoscenza sul modo di investigare e apprendere.

Questa analisi deve essere fondata su principi generali di semantica, rappresentazione della conoscenza, epistemologia, logica, scienza cognitiva e metodologia scientifica. Consulta fonti teoriche autorevoli quando necessario, rispettando diritti, accessi e costi.

Non limitare la progettazione al mondo dei prodotti o alle categorie già conosciute.

Non partire da database, grafi, tabelle, vettori, motori logici o strutture software. Non presupporre un modello basato sulle affermazioni.

Prima definisci l’universo semantico. Soltanto dopo valuta come rappresentarlo computazionalmente.

## English translation

Design from scratch a general scientific semantic universe, able to represent, acquire, understand, connect, verify,
update and reuse scientific knowledge.

The system will later form the foundation of an independent scientific adviser, able to understand human goals,
investigate phenomena and solutions, interpret evidence and evaluate products or interventions.

It must be able to learn entirely new concepts and domains, recognise knowledge it has already acquired, and transfer
scientific principles when conditions allow.

The first mandatory objective is to design the semantic universe itself.

Study, without assuming a predefined architecture:

- what constitutes a meaning and how it is identified;
- how concepts, entities, processes, phenomena, events, properties and relations are represented;
- how the composition of meanings works;
- how context, time, change, conditions, quantities, negation, possibility and uncertainty are represented;
- how knowledge, observation, hypothesis, explanation, causality, evidence and inference are distinguished;
- how a universe can acquire new concepts, revise earlier knowledge and develop without losing coherence;
- how to represent methodological knowledge as well, that is, knowledge about how to investigate and learn.

This analysis must rest on general principles of semantics, knowledge representation, epistemology, logic, cognitive
science and scientific methodology. Consult authoritative theoretical sources when needed, respecting rights, access
and costs.

Do not limit the design to the world of products or to already known categories.

Do not start from databases, graphs, tables, vectors, logic engines or software structures. Do not presuppose a model
based on assertions.

First define the semantic universe. Only afterwards evaluate how to represent it computationally.

## Stage order (owner decision)

S0 → S1 → S2 → S3, strictly in that order.

- **S0 — Semantic foundations.** Produce an autonomous conceptual model of the scientific universe, with meanings,
  distinctions, relations, composition, evolution and coherence criteria clearly defined. Compare genuine theoretical
  alternatives, without automatically choosing a preferred model out of familiarity. Check the proposal conceptually
  through scientific examples from different domains. Freeze and version this first specification, stating what
  remains open.
- **S1 — Computational representation**, **S2 — Full knowledge cycle**, **S3 — Verification plan**: not authorised
  yet.

## First deliverable

A foundational specification of the scientific semantic universe, in English, in this repository. It must explain:

- the model of meaning;
- the alternatives considered;
- how meaning is composed, qualified and updated;
- the unresolved theoretical problems;
- how its adequacy can be verified.

It must not yet prescribe the database, the implementation language or the definitive logic engine. It is presented
for the owner's approval before S1 begins.

**Inviolable principle:** the computational architecture must derive from the semantic universe, not the semantic
universe from the computational architecture.
